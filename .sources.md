- EA SPORTS FC 27 official gameplay deep dive (29 Jul 2026): https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive — EA confirms reduced automatic defensive tackles in Competitive gameplay and more context-aware curved attacking runs; it does not disclose the underlying production algorithm or model weights.
- EA SPORTS FC 25 official gameplay deep dive (25 Jul 2024): https://www.ea.com/en/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-25/news/pitch-notes-fc-25-gameplay-deep-dive — EA describes FC IQ, role-based positioning, and says a proprietary AI model uses Opta data for most Role Familiarity assignments; the exact model and its training/inference pipeline are not public.
- KONAMI eFootball v5.4 official update notes (9 Apr 2026): https://www.konami.com/efootball/en-us/topic/news/5052 — confirmed adjustments include backpedalling, delayed pressure based partly on Defensive Engagement, fewer players joining pressure at once, and marking timing.
- KONAMI eFootball v6.0 official update notes (12 Aug 2026; the cited notice lists Steam): https://www.konami.com/efootball/en-us/topic/news/5639 — confirmed adjustments include steadier marking, more space between defensive/midfield lines, more natural marking handovers, sensible cover distances, and contextual movement speeds.
- Implementation note: the game uses original, transparent role/formation steering, predicted ball/carrier positions, open-lane scoring, and a one-presser/one-cover rule inspired by public gameplay behavior. These are our own lightweight heuristics, not claims about EA/KONAMI source code or a reproduction of their proprietary AI, Opta data, animations, or assets.
- EA SPORTS FC 27 official gameplay deep dive (29 Jul 2026): https://www.ea.com/games/ea-sports-fc/fc-27/news/pitch-notes-fc27-gameplay-deep-dive — EA states that updated attacking spatial awareness helps AI players react to available space and avoid crowded areas, and that untriggered creative runs account for defensive positioning/offside; EA does not disclose the underlying algorithm. The game's teammate-occupancy scoring is an original design inference from that published behavior, not EA's method.

- IFAB, Law 10 — Determining the Outcome of a Match (2026): https://www.theifab.com/laws/latest/determining-the-outcome-of-a-match/ — Law 10.1 states the whole ball must pass over the goal line between the posts and under the crossbar for a goal.
- The FA, Law 10 — Determining the Outcome of a Match, Laws of the Game 2026–27: https://www.thefa.com/football-rules-governance/lawsandrules/laws/football-11-11/law-10---determining-the-outcome-of-a-match — independently confirms the same whole-ball crossing condition for 11v11.
- Implementation note: the game waits until the ball centre passes the physical goal line by more than one ball radius, then interpolates x/height at that score plane and evaluates the full-ball aperture and keeper there. This is our own discrete-step geometry, not a published algorithm from a commercial game.
